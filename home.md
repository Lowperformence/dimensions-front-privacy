# Dimensions Front

**Dimensions Front** is a fast-paced mobile sci-fi MOBA for Android.

Choose your hero, fight alongside your team, and battle across unique planets and dimensions. Each battlefield combines tactical team combat, distinct heroes, AI-controlled units, and a futuristic universe built for mobile play.

## Features

- Fast-paced MOBA battles
- Unique heroes with different abilities and playstyles
- Multiple planets and sci-fi battle environments
- Team-based combat and tactical objectives
- Designed for Android mobile devices
